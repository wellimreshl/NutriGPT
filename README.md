# NutriGPT

**Your personal AI-powered nutrition companion.**

NutriGPT is an AI-powered nutrition and wellness web application designed to make food tracking, nutritional awareness, and healthier decisions easier.

The goal is to bring intelligent meal logging, nutrition tracking, personalized insights, and AI-powered coaching together in one platform.

## Features

- **Food Logging:** Record meals and track daily food intake.
- **Nutrition Tracking:** Monitor calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fats.
- **Daily Dashboard:** View daily nutrition totals and progress towards personal goals.
- **AI Nutrition Assistant:** Interact with an AI assistant to make meal logging and nutrition analysis easier.
- **Personalized Insights:** Receive nutrition analysis and recommendations based on logged meals and daily goals.
- **Progress Tracking:** Monitor nutrition habits over time.

## Vision

NutriGPT aims to go beyond traditional calorie tracking by combining nutrition science, artificial intelligence, and an intuitive user experience.

The long-term vision is to build an accessible personal nutrition platform that helps people understand their eating habits and make informed decisions.

## Project Status

NutriGPT is actively being developed. New features, improvements, and refinements are part of its ongoing development.
